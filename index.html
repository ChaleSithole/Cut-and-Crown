Our story

Cut & Crown opened in 2021 on Church Street with two chairs and one idea: a haircut should be more than a routine appointment.

We blend classic barbering craft with modern styles. Today we run three chairs, keep our books open online, and still know most regulars by name.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated 1 September 2026

1. Bookings

A booking is confirmed once you complete the booking form and see the confirmation screen. Please keep your booking reference. Appointment times are reserved for the service you selected.

2. Cancellations and changes

Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment by phone or email. Repeated no-shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

3. Late arrivals

We hold your slot for 10 minutes. After that we may shorten your service or reschedule you so we don't delay other clients.

4. Pricing and payment

Prices are in South African rand (ZAR), include VAT and are paid in the shop by cash or card. Prices may change without notice, but the price shown when you book is the price you pay.

5. Offers and codes

The FIRSTCUT code gives 10% off a first appointment, one use per client, and cannot be combined with other offers.

6. Calendar events

Calendar files are provided for convenience and use the details you selected. Please check the event in your calendar. The shop's booking record is the final reference.

7. Personal information

We use your name, email and phone number only to manage your appointment. See our Privacy Policy for details.

8. Right to refuse service

We may refuse or stop service where staff safety, hygiene or respectful conduct is at risk.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page applies at the time you book.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you book we ask for your name, email, phone number and any notes you add.

How we use it

To manage your appointment and contact you about it. We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

Storage

This website keeps your recent booking details in your own browser only so that taken time slots are not offered twice on your device.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at hello@cutandcrown.co.za.
